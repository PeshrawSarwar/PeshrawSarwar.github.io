I’m a product professional with a developer’s mindset and a focus on the people behind every interaction.

My journey has taken me from building software and supporting enterprise applications to owning digital products at Asiacell. I translate customer and business needs into clear priorities, working across design, engineering, and commercial teams to bring them to life.

Now based in Germany, I’m pursuing an M.Sc. in International Information Systems at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, with a focus on Digital Business.

Beyond my work, I read widely about history and psychology, with a particular interest in psychoanalysis and game theory. I’m curious about what shapes human behavior, how people make decisions, and how technology and psychology can come together to create more thoughtful digital experiences.

I’m also interested in cybersecurity and how we can make digital systems safer and more trustworthy. I completed the Google Cybersecurity Specialization on Coursera in 2025, building a foundation in risk management, network security, threat detection, and incident response.
